# Hustlyst

**You don't need to be a tech person to work like one.**

Hustlyst is a newsletter about AI and automation tools for people who do not code. Twice a week, one tool at a time, tested before it is written about.

The readers are in sales, HR, marketing, operations and support. Freelancers, students, people running their own thing. Not developers. The question is always the same three parts: does this solve a real problem I have, can I use it without a tutorial, and is it worth the money.

## The part that makes it different

Every deep dive carries a **Before You Install** section. That means the privacy policy, the terms of service, what people are actually saying on Reddit, and whatever surfaced in the threads that the launch post did not mention.

It is not a takedown. It is the briefing a friend who already uses the tool would give you before you hand over your data or your card.

---

## What gets written about, and what does not

A tool has to clear all three of these:

- **Usable on day one by someone non-technical.** No code, no API keys, no setup weekend
- **Useful across more than one job.** If someone in sales, HR, ops and marketing cannot all find a use for it, it is too narrow
- **Solves a work or life problem, not a technical one.** Developer tooling belongs somewhere else, and there are better people to write about it

That last rule rules out a lot of what is currently loud, on purpose.
